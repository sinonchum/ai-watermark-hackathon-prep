# AI Watermark Hackathon Prep

Preparation notes for the [European AI Championship Paris](https://networkinparis.com/event/evt-t7ZboosIQPBRAB2) (20–22 November 2026). The event theme is AI watermarking: marking, detecting, and attributing AI-generated content, against the background of [EU AI Act Article 50](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/).

This repository is preparation based on public information and past tasks. It is not the official brief. The problem statements, scoring, and rules will be announced by the organizers, after Meta's opening talk on the morning of 21 November 2026. Links were checked on 16 November 2026.

The full analysis is in Chinese, written for Simon Qin, Chuan Li, Xuanhao Hu, and Wenhan Ding.
